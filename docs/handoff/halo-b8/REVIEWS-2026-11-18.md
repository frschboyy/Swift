### A3 hold during calls (`7ab2b70`): reject as is
Full report: [`../../audit/reviews/review-a3-calls-7ab2b70.md`](../../audit/reviews/review-a3-calls-7ab2b70.md). No product rule
breaks (the hold never approves, denies or reorders), but A3 misses its own goal. Mergeable once findings 1 to 3 are fixed.
Tests run by the reviewer: 277 passed, 1 skipped. No live call was tried.
1. **High:** a card that arrives during a call is still drawn (`hold_cards` hides only the card on screen when the call starts,
   `session.py:4331-4346`; later cards go out normally, `session.py:4236`). Result and notice cards are not held. Fix: send
   `confirm.hold held=True` right after `confirm.request`, and test a card arriving mid-call.
2. **High:** Store-installed call apps (new Teams, Store WhatsApp, Slack, Zoom) are never detected: `calls.py:43-47` compares
   `MSTeams_8wekyb3d8bbwe` with `ms-teams.exe`. Fix: match by install folder, or treat an open, recently started call-app entry as a call.
3. **Medium:** a stale registry entry can hold Swift for hours with no sign, and `set_call` never calls `announce()`. Fix:
   announce the hold; ignore entries started more than a few hours ago.
4. **Medium:** the owner can't bring a held card back during a call (`session.py:1466`, held again by `lifecycle.py:123`).
5. **Medium:** an error inside `hold_cards` kills the watcher silently (`calls.py:113-117`); a hold begun then stays forever. Catch around `on_change`, log the class, keep polling.
6. Low: the probe blocks the event loop ~20 ms every 2 s (use `asyncio.to_thread`). 7. `dialogue.forget()` reruns on every presence
   change during a call (run it only when the hold switches on). 8. substring matching (`msedge` matches `msedgewebview2.exe`): match exact names.
   9. a failing probe logs every 2 s: log once. 10. nit: records say "away" during a call hold.
Answers to the author's flags: `dialogue.forget()` at call start is wanted (safe direction; a pending question goes to the stack,
at the cost of "make it louder" losing its referent). Away and call together: tested both ways. `swift.log` holds no app names.
Still not built: the overlay half ("Holding 3" moon pill, "Badge · OK", "While you were in your call" digest); no live call tested
(owner check `laughing-nightingale-968e04-1`).
